## Critical safety notes

### Grey Creek Pass — Wed Jun 17
- 17 km @ 9% avg grade to 2,089 m
- 2026 snowpack above normal — snow likely at summit
- Check snow stations 2D05/2D10 the week before
- **Bailout if snow is impassable:** Hwy 3 / Kootenay Pass (Crawford Bay → Creston → Cranbrook → Kimberley). Paved, year-round.

### Bulldog Tunnel — Mon Jun 15
- 912 m pitch black, curved, sandy, wet
- **Monday = log trucks possible**. Listen at west portal, walk single file, stay right
- Strong front + rear lights required

### Fire ban — entire trip
- Penticton open-fire ban in effect since May 7, 2026
- Kamloops Fire Centre (Day 1-2 Crown land) likely escalates to full ban by mid-June
- **Plan stove-only**. CSA/ULC propane stoves still allowed under all bans.

### BC Crown land rules
- Wild-camping legal up to 14 days per spot, no permit
- Pull 50-100 m off trail, Leave No Trace, set back from water bodies
- Naramata Bench (D1 km 0-13) all PRIVATE — no camping until Glenfir/Adra zone
