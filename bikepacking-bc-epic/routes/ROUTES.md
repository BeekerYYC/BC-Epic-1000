## Key notes

- **Day 1 is the longest** (147 km) with a sustained climb out of Penticton
  to the KVR high country (~1,276 m).
- **Day 2 is the recovery day** — least climbing (559 m), net downhill along
  the Kettle Valley.
- **Day 4 ends at Gray Creek base** deliberately, setting up the Gray Creek
  Pass climb fresh on the morning of Day 5. Includes the Kootenay Lake ferry
  crossing (Balfour → Kootenay Bay) — check ferry schedule.
- **Day 5 is the queen stage**: Gray Creek Pass tops out at ~2,068 m with
  2,206 m of climbing — the highest and hardest day. Remote, no resupply
  between Gray Creek and Cranbrook side.
- **Snow risk**: Gray Creek Pass frequently holds snow into late June. Verify
  pass conditions before departure; fallback is the Highway 3 detour.
